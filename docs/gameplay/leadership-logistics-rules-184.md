## Selection/passive/synergy policy as currently implemented

Both selected passives apply faction-wide regardless of leader proximity/survival. Multipliers multiply; morale/armor add. Same character in both slots is allowed: its passive applies twice (Han Xin speed1.08², Trần Nhân Tông morale+30). No new balance approval is implied. Tags are unioned, so each matching synergy activates once, including tags supplied by one selected character; identical roles do not double a synergy. Existing synergies: United Resistance heal×1.25/armor+1/consumption×0.92; Organized Levy train×0.9; Maritime Supply naval×1.1/logistics×1.15; Rapid Columns speed×1.08; Sustained Campaign consumption×0.92. The master requires separate King/General choices and a flexible synergy architecture; it does not explicitly require different identities or forbid same-choice stacking.

## Recruitment food abstraction / physical field rations

Current strategic recruitment affordability sums finite food in ALL completed friendly Residence/Granary/Depot stores, then drains nearest-to-recruiting-building first, distance/id ties deterministic, without path or range restriction. Wagons, incomplete/dead/enemy stores are excluded. Recruits spawn with full unit foodMax independent of cargo transfer. This is an existing recruitment abstraction, not a newly authorized teleport policy. Field replenishment remains finite nearby Residence/Granary/Wagon inventory; Depot supplies wagons rather than direct unit resupply. No auto ship-food resupply.

Master section6 says food must have a real logistics chain and 'Do NOT simply teleport all frontline food.' Sections7/8 explicitly require consumed unit rations and removal from stocked Granary inventory; current field mechanics satisfy that finite transfer rule. Recruitment-locality/reservation and accounting the initial full ration against recruitment cost are not explicitly specified. G5 therefore remains an abstraction/design boundary, not silently closed as master-compliant or rebalanced. Localizing would affect queue/cancel/refund/stalled spawn/AI contracts and needs a separate finite order.

## Construction ground versus illustrated footprint

Current placement floors requested coordinates and centers them on a tile. It validates center bounds, land-passable terrain, no ford; Shipyard additionally requires water nearby within3 on both axes. Building and unit collisions use radius rectangles; neighboring terrain/bounds touched by the building radius are not individually validated. Thus a legal center can have a radius overlapping mountain/water/map edge. Movement independently blocks building interiors with radius+0.15, except own gates/self; sprite overhang is not a ground occupancy rule.

The inspected master requires buildings/terrain/logistics but gives no explicit all-covered-cells terrain rule. G6 remains recorded center-placement policy, not approval of every shoreline illustration or master override. A full-footprint rule would require coastal/spawn/navigation compatibility intake before changing runtime.
