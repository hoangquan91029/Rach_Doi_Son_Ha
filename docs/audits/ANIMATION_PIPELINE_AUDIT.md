## Root cause assessment

1. **INFERRED:** Natural-language “opposite foot” and screen-space shoe separation underconstrain physical leg ownership. A correct contact pair needs connected hip→knee→ankle→sole identity through occlusion; swapping screen foot positions is insufficient.
2. **VERIFIED from169 / INFERRED for failures:** Some guides encoded an ambiguous swing path. Knee advance, depth layering and ankle travel were not independently specified relative to each leg's own lane. The generator is not solely established as the cause.
3. **INFERRED:** Competing canonical appearance and pose references may encourage return to a familiar original stance. 486 proves a failed original-first trial; it does not prove target-first works or that ordering alone caused failure.
4. **VERIFIED distinction:** Identity retention, anatomical leg alternation, cloth continuity and temporal naturalness are separate gates. Current receipts support several identity-preserving failures. They do not support a claim that cloth deformation or eight-direction gait is generally solved.
5. **INFERRED:** Starting in-betweens before qualifying opposing contacts increases discarded work. Repeated equivalent prompts and microscope audits are inefficient; one brief actual loop plus visible corrections is the intended review method.

## Proposed reusable five-layer framework

| Layer | Reusable contract | Character-specific data / gate |
|---|---|---|
| Identity | Immutable original/master SHA, source lineage, appearance/equipment ownership, complete framing | Each same-original character; preserve costume, proportions, weapons, original shadow policy and pivot. |
| Motion | Action archetype, contact/pass phases, physical L/R support/swing IDs, normalized hip/knee/ankle/sole tracks, stance/swing windows and explicit loop | Adapt stride/amplitude to body, equipment and gait. No shared pixel frames or copied world stride. Horses, wagons, ships and leaders need their applicable archetypes. |
| Direction | Engine world direction→projected travel vector; near/far depth order independent of physical L/R | D0–D7 separately qualified, no mirror; D0 world+X projects (+1,+.5). Specify each foot relative to its own projected neutral lane. |
| Illustration | Whole connected pose guides and selected keys, bounded finite illustration calls, raw custody, cloth/equipment continuity | Canonical appearance is a lock, pose guide is a construction target. Reject obvious wrong support, missing equipment, extra limbs or camera drift; no AI guarantee. |
| Runtime | Existing compiler/schema/assembler, fixed family scale/pivot, explicit timing/events, measured stride, candidate→qualified promotion | One brief gameplay-size loop and relevant actual browser scene before approval. Simulation remains authoritative; draw counters do not prove per-actor foot lock. |

For a travel unit vector **t**, each foot's signed advance is `dot(foot − ownNeutralLane, t)`; depth is a separate occlusion label. During swing, forward ankle placement and toe direction must agree with travel; during stance, projected local sole motion must approximately counter root motion. Use heel/toe contact consistently rather than treating an ankle as a planted sole. A simple guide can expose sign/ownership errors, but numerical thresholds cannot certify realistic anatomy or natural motion.

The contract should represent ContactA → passingA → ContactB → passingB with stable physical leg IDs and depth order. Eight final illustrated frames must show a coherent exchange and loop seam; cloth follows whole-body weight shift without detached rotating parts. Reuse these phase semantics and validators across identities, then adapt the body/equipment controls. Do not recycle one unit's illustrated limbs.
