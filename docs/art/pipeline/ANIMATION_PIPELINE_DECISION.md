## Coordinates and authoring lock

WorldX/Y lie on the map; Z is up. Body forward follows headingθ; anatomical LEFT is(-sinθ,cosθ,0), RIGHT the opposite. Exact existing ground projection is screenX=27(X−Y), screenY=13.5(X+Y). Uniform export scale only; no per-frame bbox fitting. For authoring yaw, use a declared3D basis, not a front-view mannequin rotated in image space.

Camera ambiguity discovered during verification: the existing metadata combines elevation26.565° with a2:1 square-ground projection. A conventional orthographic camera at45°azimuth has ground ratio sin(elevation);26.565° gives0.4472, while0.5 requires30°. The2D renderer has no3D height projection to resolve this. The pilot retains26.565° as the legacy reference angle and declares its actual matrix; it cannot establish full exact-camera equivalence. Before second-direction fanout, require a ground calibration render and one globally declared dimetric projection or a reviewed metadata correction. ## Walk timing and contact gates

Start with eight phase keys; the number of output temporal samples is an independent decision. Local forward coordinate f and lateral l define foot tracks. Stance foot moves backward relative to root at exactly simulation-equivalent speed. Swing follows a smooth periodic return with positive clearance. Double contact, heel/toe roll and constant-bone IK must not change the ground pivot.

| Key | LEFT | RIGHT | Body phase |
|---|---|---|---|
| F0 | front contact | rear contact/toe-off | contact |
| F1 | stance retreat | lifting/forward swing | down |
| F2 | mid stance | passes stance foot | passing |
| F3 | rear stance | approaching contact | up |
| F4 | rear contact/toe-off | front contact | contact |
| F5 | lifting/forward swing | stance retreat | down |
| F6 | passes stance foot | mid stance | passing |
| F7 | approaching contact | rear stance | up |

Cycle duration=strideWorld/actualSpeed. AtD07 nominal2.1world/s projects to86.604CSSpx/s atzoom1.08. Do not combine a600ms authored loop with unrelated travel speed. Animation distance accumulates from interpolated render positions; roots carry no extra world translation in exported images. Avoid abrupt gait resets while turning; preserve distance phase and select the correctly authored heading. At stop, settle to idle with a bounded transition; an unrendered crossfade is not a replacement action. Final death is nonlooping and held on last frame; visibility/removal still comes from simulation.

Critical discrete limitation: while one sprite frame is held, its planted foot moves with the continuously moving root. Maximum hold drift≈projectedSpeed×frameDuration. At86.6px/s a75ms hold creates6.5px drift. Exact contact at sample instants does NOT pass the no-sliding gate. Do not hide it with per-frame pivots, global sprite wobble or simulation speed changes. If eight-frame playback fails, keep that failed receipt and sample the SAME motion more densely; choose N≥ceil(projectedSpeed×cycleMs/(1000×2px)). Twelve may improve smoothness but is not assumed sufficient. Higher temporal count has higher atlas cost; measure before locking production budgets.

VI: Tám mốc dáng không đồng nghĩa chỉ cần tám ảnh phát lại. Khi mô phỏng vẫn di chuyển mà sprite giữ nguyên một ảnh, chân trụ có thể trượt nhiều pixel. Phải đo vòng phát thật; nếu thất bại, tăng số mẫu thời gian của cùng chuyển động, không đổi pivot, lắc sprite hoặc giảm tốc độ game để che lỗi.

- Continuous contact residual≤0.01model units; actual60Hz held-sprite planted-toe excursion≤2CSSpx per stance hold at80px and measured travel speed. Test64/96 too and report each result.

- Bone length residual≤1e−5 relative; foot penetration≤0.01model; continuous periodic pose endpoint residual≤1e−5. Ground pivot invariant; deliberate torso bob is allowed, unexplained image-fit drift is not.

## Scale math and loading

Count unique rendered identities U, directions D, frames per direction A=sum(actionFrames). SourceFrames=U×D×A, with action/direction exceptions summed separately. Shared clip reduces authoring work but does NOT reduce identity-specific baked frames. For128² runtime cells +4px total gutter, a2048² atlas fits15²=225frames. AtlasCount=ceil(totalFrames/225), with extra fragmentation for action/family loading. RGBAbytes=atlasCount×2048²×4; fullmips≈×4/3. Download is measured compressed bytes, not GPU memory.

The crop origin and translated ground pivot must be identical across every frame; never fit each bbox. A typical64×128 humanoid runtime cell plus4px gutter fits30×15=450 cells per2048 atlas, twice the square-cell capacity. This is a packing estimate; actual union includes weapons and may be wider. Keep original source canvas/custody intact.

Dense temporal art makes compressed GPU textures worth measuring in the eventual required WebGL backend. Staged banks, global crop and compression address volume; lowering motion thresholds does not.

Menu loads UI/portraits only. Lobby loads chosen leaders, villagers and common actions first; buildings/terrain/map are separate banks. Load roster on demand before units become visible; no midcombat missing-art substitution marked accepted. Retain both sides' necessary assets. Low animation LOD may reduce sampling of breathing/VFX at distance, never remove movement contact correctness or impact cues.
