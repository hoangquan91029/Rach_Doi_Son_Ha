# Main Residence art pilot

Two separate, original built-in ImageGen outputs are preserved as **raw candidates**. Neither is accepted, selected, cleaned, or connected to runtime code. Parent review is pending; this pilot does not approve a production asset or a larger art batch.

| Candidate | Preview at 128 × 128 source-canvas size | Visible bounds at alpha ≥ 128 | Preliminary visual note |
|---|---|---:|---|
| `variant-01/` | `variant-01/preview-game-scale-128.png` | 1175 × 935 px in the 1254 × 1254 raw image | Detailed two-storey compound, but the ridge sculptures lean toward the generic East Asian palace look the prompt excluded. Review as a concern, not a selection. |
| `variant-02/` | `variant-02/preview-game-scale-128.png` | 1208 × 926 px in the 1254 × 1254 raw image | Lower timber hall with a broad tiled roof and exposed posts. Still requires parent assessment for historical fit, scale, style, and runtime integration. |

Each variant folder keeps its verbatim prompt, unmodified raw image, generated-image source path, hash and alpha metadata, and transparent and game-scale previews. The preview composites the complete square source onto a plain moss-jade field so transparent edges and gameplay-scale visibility can be inspected; it is not an in-game screenshot.

The built-in tool returned 1254 × 1254 RGBA PNGs despite the prompts specifying a 1024 × 1024 source canvas. Raw outputs remain unchanged. No runtime resizing, masking, alpha cleanup, or placement adjustment has been applied. At a 128 × 128 preview canvas, the candidate subjects occupy about 120–123 px in width. Exact in-game anchor and tile-footprint alignment remains unverified.

## Research anchors

The design brief consulted the Imperial Citadel of Thăng Long’s official account of Kinh Thiên Palace and its 2025 excavation report, plus UNESCO’s description of the Huế monument complex. These support timber palace construction, raised stone and column foundations, connected halls, formal spatial axes, and site relationships. They do not prove that either candidate reconstructs a specific historical building.

- [Kinh Thiên Palace — Imperial Citadel of Thăng Long](https://hoangthanhthanglong.vn/en/kinh-thien-palace-the-heart-of-the-imperial-citadel-of-thang-long/)
- [2025 archaeological excavation at the Kinh Thiên Palace foundation](https://hoangthanhthanglong.vn/en/preliminary-report-on-the-results-of-the-2025-archaeological-excavation-at-the-foundation-area-of-kinh-thien-palace/)
- [Complex of Huế Monuments — UNESCO World Heritage Centre](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/678)
