# South illustrated bitmap rig contract (v2, preparation only)

This v2 sibling corrects the v1 draft’s unsupported fixed near/far labels. The original [v1 contract](part-contract.json) and [v1 README](README.md) remain unchanged for audit. No art calls are authorized by either draft.

The proposed topology remains nine original illustrated textures: one rigid identity core; two components for each arm, with each hand integrated into its lower arm; and two components for each leg, with each sandal integrated into its lower leg. Each lower-leg texture must provide separately sealed shin and foot/sandal mesh regions with a shared ankle seam and independently measured ankle, heel, toe, and sole-plane landmarks. This lets stance keep the heel and toe planted while the shin changes angle without adding separate foot textures. If polygons or landmarks are missing or ambiguous, the baker must refuse the part.

Ownership labels are anatomical only. In the front-facing South reference, actor LEFT appears viewer-right and actor RIGHT appears viewer-left; that screen position does not establish near/far depth. The two neutral feet are modeled at the same depth unless direct measured source evidence and parent review show otherwise. Resolve limb order per pose from model-space depth and actual projected overlap; crossings may change the order. The actor-LEFT sash knot/tail stays on viewer-right. Nothing may be mirrored. Hide seams under authored overlap at shoulders, hips, elbows, and knees, with core masks closing proximal seams.

Identity and camera authority is the parent-sealed South lock in `identity/identity-lock.json`; primary South source SHA-256 is `68a7fb9a71ae614f7c01432b1270e7a25071975667cfdf082ae02326d97678db`. Its status is an identity candidate for South authoring, not final-art approval. Keep the 1254×1254 canvas, between-feet source pivot (654,1140), dimetric 2:1 projection at 26.565°, and 38px whole-canvas gameplay scale. For every component, record one immutable measured uniform source-to-rest calibration. Never anisotropically calibrate the source texture or fit its bounding box per frame. Constant model-space bone lengths may have explicitly declared screen-space foreshortening from the fixed 3D camera-plane projection.

Natural rest landmarks, overlap extents, mesh boundaries, and component calibrations remain pending. Measure them from each actual original and record source-pixel coordinates, method, uncertainty, byte hash, and decoded RGBA hash. Mark hidden pivots as authored hypotheses. This contract supplies no fabricated coordinates or transforms.

The final game receives finished, complete illustrated PNG frames baked from the internal authoring rig. It never receives independently rotating component sprites or bones. The first motion review remains four complete illustrated key cels F0/F2/F4/F6, after separate parent authorization and actual-component calibration. Existing F0 is a selected candidate and F2 attempts remain held; there is no approved cycle.

`part-contract-v2.json` records exact component ownership and the explicit per-pose depth rule. This is a preparation proposal, not an image-call work order.
